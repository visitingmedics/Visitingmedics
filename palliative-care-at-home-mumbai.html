Supportive care at home

Palliative care focuses on comfort, symptom support and helping families manage care needs. Home support may be considered for patients living with serious or advanced illness when the home setting is appropriate.

What home support may involve

Depending on the patient’s needs and existing medical plan, support may include basic nursing care, medication assistance, comfort measures, wound-care support and coordination with the treating clinician.

For patients and families

Families can share the patient’s current condition, medical instructions, medicines and location so the appropriate home-care option can be discussed before arranging a visit.

When hospital care is needed

Some symptoms and emergencies cannot be safely managed at home. If the patient has severe breathing difficulty, chest pain, uncontrolled bleeding, loss of consciousness or another emergency, seek urgent hospital care rather than waiting for a home visit.
